# Product

## Register

product

## Users

This product serves Hong Kong and US stock learners, technical-analysis students, and self-directed traders who need a structured way to study indicators, market regimes, TradingView strategies, and risk controls before placing a trade. They are often comparing signals across charts, checking whether a backtest is credible, and trying to turn loose indicator knowledge into a repeatable pre-trade workflow.

## Product Purpose

The site is a Traditional Chinese technical-analysis research lab. It turns indicator definitions, expert research, Yahoo OHLCV examples, TradingView Strategy Tester cases, and Pine Script teaching templates into a local, inspectable learning system. Success means the reader can distinguish education from trade advice, see what evidence is missing, understand each strategy's settings and limitations, and avoid treating high PF backtests as proof of live profitability.

## Brand Personality

Calm, evidence-led, protective.

The voice should feel like a careful trading mentor in Hong Kong Chinese: practical, direct, and skeptical of overconfident claims. It should avoid hype, profit promises, and vague textbook language.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a crypto signal sales page, a generic AI SaaS dashboard, or a TradingView affiliate link farm. Avoid dark neon trading clichés, fake precision, guaranteed-return copy, copied strategy screenshots, external-link collections, and pages that treat public PF snippets as validated backtests.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before excitement: every metric, strategy, and source needs a visible confidence state.
2. Local first: strategy cases, settings, templates, and summaries should live in the site rather than depending on outbound links.
3. Teach the workflow: pages should help users inspect setup, signal, risk, exit, and invalidation instead of memorizing indicators.
4. Protect the learner: historical PF, win rate, and backtests must be framed as parameter-sensitive and not investment advice.
5. Dense but readable: the interface should support scanning, comparison, and repeated study without marketing-style filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, focus states, semantic landmarks, and keyboard access. Use Traditional Chinese that wraps naturally on mobile. Do not rely on color alone for source status, risk level, or validation state. Motion should be brief, purposeful, and disabled or reduced under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
