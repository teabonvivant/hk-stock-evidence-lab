# 網站分頁圖片生成提示詞

用途：為「技術指標研究所」本地網站生成高清、真實、實物感圖片。每個提示詞都獨立設計，不重複場景。所有圖片應避免可讀文字、品牌 logo、平台商標、投資承諾或誤導性買賣訊號。

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Subject: an open laptop showing an abstract candlestick chart interface with colored moving-average lines, a paper notebook with neatly drawn trading-plan boxes, a mechanical pencil, a calculator, and a small stack of index cards.  
Style/medium: realistic editorial photography, premium financial education mood, not a screenshot, not a UI mockup.  
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape, laptop and notebook placed slightly right of center, generous clean negative space on the left for website text, shallow depth of field but chart and notebook edges still sharp.  
Lighting/mood: bright natural morning light, soft highlights, balanced exposure, optimistic but professional, no dark trading-room mood.  
Color palette: warm white, muted teal, graphite gray, small amber highlights, realistic paper texture.  
Materials/textures: brushed metal laptop, matte paper, glass window reflections, pencil graphite, crisp printed grid lines.  
Constraints: no readable words, no logos, no people, no watermark, no exaggerated profit arrows, no currency piles.
